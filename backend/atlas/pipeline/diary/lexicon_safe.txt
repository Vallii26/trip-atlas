# Atlas safe narrative vocabulary — version 1
# Words a generated diary sentence may use WITHOUT evidence. Everything else must be licensed by
# the cited evidence (payload values, the traveller's note) or by a lexicon entry that verifies.
# One word per whitespace-separated token; inflections (-s, -ed, -ing) are accepted automatically.
# Rule for adding a word: it must assert nothing about the world on its own. No adjectives of
# size, quality, speed or mood; no negation; no "again/still/finally/usual" (they imply history).
# version: 1

# generic motion / progress verbs
start began begin begun head go went gone get got reach arrive leave left come came stop end
finish spend spent take took taken make made continue follow cross move turn pull carry led lead
bring brought set kept keep stay last passed passing

# perception / reporting verbs (the object still has to be licensed)
look saw see seen watch say says said wrote write note notes words word record recorded

# neutral nouns
day days time way place stop part trip journey ride route leg stretch side point rest thing
total distance while moment

# neutral connectives / adverbs
there here also too straight onward onwards
